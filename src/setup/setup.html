Apple Music needs one quick step

Apple Music only plays inside apps signed by castLabs, the company that makes its copy-protection plug-in. Signing is free, but it needs a castLabs account.

Make one here and The Incinerator signs itself. You only do this once.

Only want your MP3s? Skip for now. You can set up Apple Music later from the SONGS drawer.
